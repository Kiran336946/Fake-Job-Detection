ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Making Online Job Searching Safer

Fake Job Detection is a web-based platform designed to help job seekers identify potentially fraudulent job advertisements before they apply.

OUR PURPOSE

Why We Created Fake Job Detection

Finding a job online has become an important part of modern career searching. However, fake job advertisements and recruitment scams can make this process risky.

Our project aims to provide job seekers with a simple way to analyze suspicious job descriptions and identify common warning signs associated with fraudulent postings.

The platform focuses on text-based job verification and presents the analysis in a simple and understandable way.

🛡️

Our Goal

To help users make safer and more informed decisions when searching and applying for jobs online.

OUR MISSION

What Our Platform Does

We combine a simple interface with intelligent analysis to improve awareness of fake job scams.
🔍

Analyze

Analyze the text of a job advertisement for suspicious terms and patterns.

📊

Identify Risk

Highlight potential warning signs and provide an understandable risk indication.

🛡️

Protect

Increase awareness and encourage users to verify employers before applying.
